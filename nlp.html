NLP & Health AI

Natural language processing and sentiment analysis of public health discourse on social media

Overview

Between 2022 and 2023, this line of work applied text mining, sentiment classification, and deep learning to public discussion of health topics on Twitter, including COVID-19 vaccination and the 2022 monkeypox (mpox) outbreak. The studies built and analyzed large tweet datasets to characterize public sentiment and emotional reactions and to model vaccine hesitancy.

The work complements the group’s signal-based research by studying a different source of health information: unstructured text generated by the public.
